Using Foodalise

Foodalise lets you photograph, score and share the dishes you eat at restaurants. You need to be 13 or older to make an account. Keep your password safe; you are responsible for what is posted from your account.

Your posts

You own the photos, scores and reviews you post. By posting them you let Foodalise store, show and share them in the app, and use them to promote Foodalise, without paying you. This stops when you delete the post or your account, apart from copies other people already shared or short-lived backups.

Be fair and honest

Only review dishes you actually ate, and give your honest opinion. Don't post fake reviews, including reviews of your own restaurant or a rival's. Only post photos you took or have permission to use. If you got the dish free or were paid, say so in your review. Our Reviews policy (in Settings) has the details.

Not allowed

Nothing offensive, hateful, sexual, violent or illegal. No harassing or threatening anyone, no sharing other people's personal details, no spam or adverts, and no photos that aren't about food or the restaurant.

Reports and removal

You can report any review or block any person. We check reports and may remove posts or close accounts that break these rules, without warning if needed.
