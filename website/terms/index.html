Foodalise lets you photograph, score and share the dishes you eat at restaurants. You need to be 18 or older to make an account. Keep your password safe; you are responsible for what is posted from your account.

By posting them you let Foodalise store, show and share them in the app, and use them to promote Foodalise, without paying you. This stops when you delete the post or your account, apart from copies other people already shared or short-lived backups.

Be fair and honest

Only review dishes you actually ate, and give your honest opinion. Don't post fake reviews, including reviews of your own restaurant or a rival's. Opinions are fine, but don't state things as facts that aren't true (for example, that a restaurant made you ill when it didn't). Only post photos you took or have permission to use. If you got the dish free or were paid, say so in your review. Our Reviews policy (in Settings) has the details.

No harassing or threatening anyone, no pretending to be someone else, no sharing other people's personal details, no photos of other people without their permission, no spam or adverts, and no photos that aren't about food or the restaurant.

Our team checks every report, aiming to do so within 2 working days, and removes content that breaks these terms. We may remove posts, reset profiles, or suspend or close accounts that break them, without warning if needed, and we report content to the police or other authorities when the law requires it. We apply these rules in the same way to everyone.
